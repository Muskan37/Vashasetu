Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Vashasetu ("the app," "we," "us") is a regional Bangla dialect translator. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled.

Information we collect

  • Account information: if you create an account, we store your name, email address, and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in readable form).
  • Translation history: if you're logged in, the text you translate and its translated result are saved to your account so you can view them later. If you're not logged in, this is stored anonymously against your browser session only.
  • Dictionary contributions: words you submit to grow the dialect dictionary, along with your account name (or "Anonymous" if you're not logged in), are stored for admin review.
  • Text you translate: sent to the app's own server to be translated, and saved in your history (your account, or your browser session if you are a guest). It is not sent to any third-party translation service.
  • Preferred dialect: a setting you choose, stored against your account.
  • Game progress: your day streak and best quiz score are kept on your own device (in your browser's storage). We do not receive or store them.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your data to any third party.
  • We do not use your translations or contributions for advertising.
  • We do not share your account information with anyone outside of operating the app itself.

Photos you scan for text

If you use the camera button to read text from a picture, the image is processed on your own device, inside your browser. The picture is not uploaded to our servers and is not stored. Only the text that is read from it (which you can edit) is used, in the same way as text you type.

Third-party services

Voice input and voice output (speech-to-text and text-to-speech) are provided by your device's own browser (the Web Speech API). Audio you speak is processed by your browser/operating system's speech service, not stored or transmitted by Vashasetu itself.

Data retention & deletion

Your translation history and account remain stored until you delete them. You can delete individual translation history entries at any time within the app. To request full account deletion, contact us using the details below.

Children's privacy

Vashasetu is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to: vashasetu@gmail.com
